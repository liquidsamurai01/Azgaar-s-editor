# Historical Accuracy Checklist

Before a scene is canon:
- [ ] Is the real-history background before the POD correct (dates, names, titles, geography)?
- [ ] Technology, weapons, medicine, travel speed plausible for the date?
- [ ] Money, prices, and units fit the era and place?
- [ ] Language, idiom, forms of address, naming customs?
- [ ] Religion, class, gender, and legal norms plausible and consistent?
- [ ] Climate, season, weather, daylight for that date and place?
- [ ] Who knows what, and when, given communication speed?
- [ ] Does the divergence logic hold, or is it a leftover from real history?
- [ ] Sensitivity: real people, atrocities, living descendants handled deliberately?
